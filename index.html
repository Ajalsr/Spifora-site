Inside the app

Everything the desktop client runs

One binary, one backend — every module ships in the same download. Your license key sets which ones your organization can use.

Inventory

Items, warehouses, stock adjustments and valuation, synced with every sale and purchase.

Sales

Quotes, sales orders, invoices, credit notes and payments received — all traceable to one customer.

Purchasing

Purchase orders, vendor bills, GRNs, vendor credits and payments made.

Finance

Chart of accounts, journal entries, bank reconciliation, multi-currency, VAT and financial reports.

Reports & Dashboard

Sales, purchase, inventory, aging, VAT and financial-statement reports plus a live dashboard.

HR & Payroll

Employee directory, org chart, salary structures, pay runs with GL posting, payslips and time-off.

Role-based access control is built into every plan — it applies regardless of which modules you use.
