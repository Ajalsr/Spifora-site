Inside the app

Everything the desktop client runs

Spifora is one binary talking to one backend — every module below ships in the same download. Which ones your organization can actually use is set by your license key, chosen when the organization is created.

Inventory

Items, warehouses, stock adjustments and valuation, kept in sync with every sale and purchase.

Sales

Quotes, sales orders, invoices, credit notes and payments received — all traceable to one customer.

Purchasing

Purchase orders, vendor bills, GRNs, vendor credits and payments made.

Finance

Chart of accounts, journal entries, bank reconciliation, multi-currency, VAT and financial reports.

Reports & Dashboard

Sales, purchase, inventory, aging, VAT and financial-statement reports plus the live dashboard.

HR & Payroll

Employee directory, org chart, salary structures, pay runs with GL posting, payslips, and paid/unpaid time-off with manager approval.

Project

Project tracking tied to your other records — tasks, timelines and status, kept in the same backend as everything else.

Role-based access control (who on your team can do what) is built into every plan — it's not a module, it applies regardless.
